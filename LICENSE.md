# E2R-SPEC licensing map

This repository uses the following role-based licensing policy for
project-authored material. This map does not relicense third-party or imported
material, and it does not grant trademark, official-status, conformance, or
patent rights.

## Documentation and specification prose

Project-authored prose in `spec/`, `extensions/`, `docs/`, research Markdown,
and session logs is licensed under **CC BY 4.0**. The authoritative legal text
is the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.txt).
Reuse must preserve attribution and indicate modifications as required by that
license.

## Code-like specification artifacts

Standalone JSON Schemas and code-like specification artifacts in `schemas/` and
`scripts/` are licensed under **MIT**. The authoritative canonical text is the
[SPDX MIT License text](https://spdx.org/licenses/MIT.html).

## Project-created examples and data

Eligible project-created Dataset examples in `examples/**/*.json`, eligible
research JSON fixtures, and the E2R Self-Description dogfood Dataset are
dedicated under **CC0 1.0**, only where the project can grant the necessary
rights. The authoritative legal text is the [Creative Commons CC0 1.0 legal
code](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode.txt).

Third-party facts, expressive text, translations, names, logos, imported data,
and other material with separate rights are excluded from this dedication and
retain their applicable source terms. The file role alone is not evidence that
all contents are project-created.

## Provenance and canonicality

Sample-specific authorship, source references, translation status, and
redistribution readiness are recorded in
`docs/public-sample-provenance.md`. Canonicality and official E2R designation
are governance properties and are not granted by these licenses.

This repository-level map formalizes the accepted E2R standards licensing
policy. Software repositories in the E2R ecosystem have separate license
checkpoints.
