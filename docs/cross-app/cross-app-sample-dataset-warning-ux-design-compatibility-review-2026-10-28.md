### Shared classification rule to design, not yet an implementation contract

Keep the **Validator output** as the technical record: every actual Code, Severity and Path stays accessible in context. The **ordinary layer** explains a verified effect on work and its safe next action. Scope it to Current Dataset, Candidate, or Operation; a failed Import/Handoff/Export or persistence risk must not be buried under a neutral Extension explanation. An exact known ID with a matching exact version is distinct from a truly unknown ID, unsupported version, missing/conflicting declaration, or invalid payload. Neither app may infer semantic validation, editing capability, or guaranteed round-trip preservation from recognition alone. No automatic Dataset rewrite, warning filtering, severity conversion or validation bypass is proposed. UI components and state machinery remain application-local.

For the known valid sample pair under Validator `0.7.0`, the proposed ordinary treatment is restrained: state that the Dataset opened and that Dimension Assessment `0.2.0` is not edited in this app, while making the Validator's recognition limit and exact two warnings discoverable in technical detail. If an operation cannot establish preservation, or an error/refusal occurs, show the actual impact and recovery at that operation. Do not reuse the known-case text for a genuinely unknown Extension or an unsupported/mismatched version. This paragraph is a **review proposal**, not approved EN/JA copy or an authorization to change either UI.

Human decisions before Runtime work:

For each, test official EN/JA samples, a truly unknown Extension, unsupported and mismatched versions, malformed known data, mixed warnings and real errors; preserve Import/Handoff refusal, Current/Candidate attribution, Cancel, save/Export refusal and actual preservation-risk notices. Review keyboard and narrow-screen disclosure, then obtain separate Human Browser Acceptance. A Validator change requires its own release and compatibility approval before either app adopts it.

Both tracks can start design and preparation at the same time; neither waits for the other's implementation or acceptance. Their shared review compares evidence and scenarios only.
