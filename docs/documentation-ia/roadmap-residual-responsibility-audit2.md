## Classification key

- **KEEP AS CURRENT PLANNING** — current status, priority, dependency, active
  or explicitly deferred work, evidence gate, qualified acceptance, or pending
  Human decision.
- **COMPRESS TO AUTHORITY POINTER** — a dedicated result, decision, audit, or
  acceptance holds detailed evidence; keep the current consequence and link.
- **MOVE TO ROADMAP HISTORY** — chronology no longer constrains current
  planning, but the dated progression should remain as historical evidence.
- **COMPRESS TO RESEARCH POINTER** — exploratory content has a dedicated
  Research authority; retain any current planning boundary and link.
- **HOLD / MIXED** — current and historical responsibility is interleaved, or
  status/current authority needs reconciliation before safe compression.
