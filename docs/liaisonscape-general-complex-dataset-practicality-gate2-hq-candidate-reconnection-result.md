## Quality and responsibility interpretation

The recent candidate is better than B on the dimension it actually owns:
usable Node geometry, extent growth, and some occupied-bound collision
reduction. It does not own the remaining failures:

- Structural Placement: avoidable body/label packing and extent compression
  can improve; it cannot guarantee final route ownership or label association;
- ordinary routing: route shape and foreign-route interaction remain open;
- Relation-label presentation: final label placement, clearance, and
  ownership remain the strongest residual in dense and connected cases;
- Node-label placement: measured as feedback but remains Product-authoritative;
- endpoint-plan / Parallel / Incident: unchanged and not implicated by this
  smoke; controls remain bounded only, not accepted;
- Self-loop: only a retained control; independent Self-loop research remains
  separate;
- coupling: one Node-geometry candidate cannot guarantee ownership corridors
  when Product-authoritative routes and labels share a connected core.

The result also rejects an obsolete interpretation: lower overview fit, larger
extent, or smaller compactness alone is not a failure on the infinite canvas.
Fit and viewport are camera diagnostics. The dense failure is instead the
visible local route/label association defect at native Product presentation.

## Release decision and next position

The recent candidate is `PROMISING FOR GEOMETRY / NOT RELEASE-PRACTICAL`.
Fast remains a usable start. The current Product Auto Layout action remains a
poor HQ control. No recent candidate has demonstrated all of:

- clear improvement over Fast in difficult cases;
- no gross Actual Product visual defect;
- bounded practical total wall time; and
- a Product-authoritative presentation suitable for release-oriented use.

"all Initial Layout research must continue." It is specifically the absence
of one recent HQ candidate with both readable Actual Product output and a
credible execution budget. The old Product Auto Layout button is itself a
release concern if presented as the HQ action, but that concern does not
invalidate the research lineage or authorize replacing the button now.
