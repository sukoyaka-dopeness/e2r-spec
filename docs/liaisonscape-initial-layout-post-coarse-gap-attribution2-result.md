## Controlled comparison

The existing diagnostic `geometry-inspection1` seam was audited and its
comparison boundary was reused. It selects `current`,
`coarse-objective-prototype-v1`, or `post-structural-relaxation-v1`, replaces
only coordinate values in an in-memory Dataset clone, and then renders the
clone through the same `App` path. Routing, Relation-label derivation,
viewport fit, selection, and interaction are therefore downstream-common
stages for the candidate cells. No Dataset is persisted.

The Post coordinates remain research materialization evidence and are
fixture-bound in the diagnostic seam. The coarse coordinates are generated by
the bounded coarse provider. They are not treated as equivalent search
strategies or as interchangeable authorities.

## Evidence synthesis

### Node geometry signal

- Post was preferred over current in the earlier Actual Product comparison
  cells.
- Coarse produced natural, usable Node placement and modestly improved its own
  geometric proxy on Lighthouse and Titanic.
- Direct current-versus-coarse Actual Product observation on Lighthouse and
  Titanic EN/JA found no consistent human-observable coarse advantage.
- Apollo coarse unsafe-candidate cells returned current placement, so they
  provide non-regression evidence only.

Therefore, Node geometry alone does not explain the full human-observed Post
preference. The coarse proxy can capture some occupancy/spacing direction, but
the evidence is insufficient to claim that it captures the causal Post signal.

### Downstream presentation coupling

The Post objective/search evaluates signals that interact with routed Edges,
Relation-label corridors, parallel-edge pressure, and subsequent label/route
feedback. The Actual Product renderer then derives those stages from each
candidate's Node positions. This makes the final visual gap a coupled result:

```text
Node positions -> routing / obstacle recovery -> Relation-label placement
               -> Self-loop / connector presentation -> rendered visual result
```

The controlled seam keeps the right-hand stages common, but it does not make
their contribution disappear. The observed Post preference alongside mixed
coarse preference is consistent with downstream amplification or attenuation
of Node-position differences.

Known Titanic parallel-edge/long-label behavior, Self-loop angle tendency, and
connector/label rendering remain presentation tracks. They are attribution
signals, not fixes or coarse failures in this checkpoint.

### Locale

EN and JA use the same topology but different text geometry. The final
renderer derives locale-specific Node and Relation labels from the selected
positions. The observed EN/JA outputs remained usable, but the evidence does
not isolate whether a difference came from Node geometry or text/layout
derivation. Locale therefore reinforces the presentation-coupled conclusion;
it does not justify cross-locale coordinate reuse.

```text
research direction                    = PRESENTATION-COUPLED

```

The practical priority should move toward controlled routing/
Relation-label/presentation attribution research rather than further local
tuning of the parked coarse objective. If Initial Layout research resumes,
the next hypothesis must explicitly identify which presentation-coupled signal
can be approximated cheaply and boundedly; no such new proxy is introduced
here.

No additional human review is required to change this direction: the existing
three-way Actual Product evidence plus the current/coarse direct observations
are sufficient to show a mixed/insufficient coarse result. A future claim of
Node-geometry signal strength would require a new controlled human study.
