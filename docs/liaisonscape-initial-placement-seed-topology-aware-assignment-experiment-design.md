total or partial orders remain diagnostic controls. No policy is selected for

## Design scope and definition

At the tested boundary, an assignment rule is **topology-aware** when its
semantic Node classification depends only on graph-isomorphism-invariant
topology features already accepted for these controls and preserves
topology-supported equivalence classes. A literal representative selected
inside an unresolved class is operational, not semantic.

This definition intentionally excludes candidate geometry. Candidate geometry
enters a separate role-classification layer, and the mapping between the two
layers enters a separately named correspondence hypothesis.

The three responsibilities are:

| Responsibility | Question | Allowed authority |
|---|---|---|
| Topology role classification | What graph-derived role does a Node occupy? | accepted graph topology only |
| Candidate geometric role classification | What exact role does a point have inside FP1? | deterministic candidate geometry only |
| Assignment policy | Which role correspondence is admissible or materialized? | an explicit bounded hypothesis, not an implicit preference |

The design does not ask for the best assignment, replace lexical ordering in
Production, sort by more metrics, or optimize a quality score.

## Topology role inputs and equivalence

The future diagnostic may use only these existing accepted fields:

- distinct-neighbor degree;
- sorted neighbor-degree multiset;
- accepted motif membership count;
- overlap-cluster membership;
- shared motif-neighbor count;
- motif-neighbor degree multiset;
- boundary/context incident count;
- component membership; and
- existing bounded orbit/equivalence evidence.

Labels, names, locale, UUID spelling, input-array order, stored or manual
coordinates, routes, Presentation, visual state, and semantic guesses are
forbidden inputs. PageRank, betweenness, eigenvector centrality, community
detection, embeddings, machine learning features, and new graph-centrality
suites are also out of scope.

A signature may produce singleton classes, equivalent classes, a partial order,
or incomparable classes. It need not produce a total order. Lexical fallback
may remain an explicitly operational materializer detail only after all
topology-derived fields have been exhausted; it must not become a semantic
role.

Lighthouse and Ashen have distinguishable bounded roles under the accepted
refined signatures. K3 has one topology-equivalent orbit containing all three
Nodes. The experiment must not turn that orbit into three named semantic
roles.

## Candidate geometric roles and equivalence

For FP1, the future diagnostic may record:

- candidate index and normalized point;
- endpoint/interior status;
- ordinal position along the construction;
- predecessor/successor spacing;
- sorted distance profile;
- exact local chord/curvature relations where useful;
- convex-boundary role; and
- any exact candidate-set isometry/equivalence evidence.

FP1 is the one-sided convex parabola segment
`P_i=(i/(N-1),(i/(N-1))^2)`. Its construction order is not a quality order.
For the real N=10 controls, the endpoint/interior partition is descriptive,
and local spacing increases along the segment. The parabola has no non-trivial
reflection or rigid symmetry that exchanges its full finite point set, so the
exact candidate indices are not collapsed into rigid-transform equivalence
classes. Endpoint/interior is a coarse role class, not a desirability ranking.

Rigid-transform invariants may support structural comparison. Non-uniform
affine transforms change the FP1 shape descriptors and therefore cannot be
silently treated as candidate equivalence. No candidate is called center,
balanced, important, good, or bad in the future experiment.

## Assignment responsibility decomposition

The future run must preserve this order of responsibility:

1. derive topology role classes and orbit information;
2. derive candidate geometric role classes without quality language;
3. state a role-to-candidate correspondence hypothesis before materialization;
4. describe an admissible mapping set or bounded diagnostic witnesses; and
5. materialize literal coordinates only as an operational diagnostic.

The exact hard-safety relation remains the set of injective mappings satisfying
GEO-0 and GEO-3. Topology classification does not replace that relation, and a
deterministic witness does not become structural truth merely because it is
repeatable.

## Topology-order and candidate-order ambiguity

No universal source-grounded total order exists in the current evidence. The
previous signatures distinguish bounded real roles, but field priority remains
ambiguous: degree-first and motif-responsibility-first can produce different
literal witnesses. This is not hidden or resolved by lexical fallback.

Candidate index order is likewise only construction order. FP1's increasing
local spacing is exact descriptive geometry, but the evidence does not define
which direction should receive a higher topology responsibility. The future
experiment must either compare both predeclared directions or keep the mapping
set-valued. It must not call one direction better without a structural
objective.

## Role-to-candidate correspondence hypotheses

The smallest testable hypothesis is:

> **RC-H2:** when a topology role participates in more accepted
> motif-neighbor relationships, a diagnostic correspondence may test whether
> assigning it to a candidate role with greater exact local spacing/fan-out
> opportunity changes the two-layer profile and reduces representation-
> dependent profile-class splitting, compared with a predeclared reverse
> correspondence.

“Greater local spacing/fan-out opportunity” is a deterministic geometric
descriptor, not a visual preference or scalar score. The reverse correspondence
must be tested as a counter-hypothesis. The experiment succeeds by clarifying
whether the correspondence is supported, contradicted, or trade-off/mixed; it
does not succeed by naming a winner.

If the profile cannot distinguish the correspondence effects, report
`PROFILE INSUFFICIENT` and select criteria research. If topology constraints
still leave multiple profile classes, report that structural assignment
objective or candidate-family research remains open. Assignment must not be
made to succeed by definition.

## Strong rename, input order, and K3 controls

For each real graph, freeze one deterministic strong rename before execution:

1. group Nodes by distinct-neighbor degree;
2. sort each group by original ID only to define the transformation;
3. assign synthetic IDs in the reverse order within each same-degree group;
4. preserve every endpoint reference through the bijection; and
5. change no semantic name or label in the diagnostic topology.

This reverses lexical order inside selected same-degree groups while preserving
the graph. It is a disposable fixture transformation; canonical samples remain
untouched. The run must compare topology-signature transport, anonymous
profile-class transport, and any literal representative change separately.

Retain an Entity-array and Relation-array reorder control. Semantic
classification must not depend on input order; an operational witness may need
a deterministic fallback inside an unresolved class.

K3 must be evaluated at EQV-REL and EQV-CLASS/orbit level. A literal K3
representative may differ under rename or reorder, but no named Node may be
declared semantically entitled to candidate 0, 1, or 2.

## Structural profile and trade-off handling

The accepted two-layer profile remains authoritative:

- motif-local side scale, side ratios, normalized altitude, angle profile,
  bottleneck vertex/opposite pair, and exact GEO-0 state; and
- overlap-cluster shared Nodes, fan-out capacity, circular gap profile,
  narrowest-gap identity, bottleneck distribution, overlap multiplicity,
  boundary/context inventory, and exact GEO-3 state.

Profiles are compared field by field and then by complete structural profile
class modulo graph-isomorphism transport, accepted orbit renaming,
rigid-transform invariance, and permitted representation normalization. A
deterministic encoding of all fields may be used as a machine key, but it is
not a semantic scalar score.

Ashen's known trade-offs remain a first-class outcome. A motif relief paired
with another motif worsening, a moved bottleneck, or changed fan-out is
`TRADE-OFF` or `MIXED`; it is never collapsed into a winner. The future run
must report `PROFILE-CLASS-STABLE`, `PROFILE-CLASS-SPLIT`,
`LEXICAL-SENSITIVE`, `TOPOLOGY-CLASS-STABLE`, `ORBIT-EQUIVALENT`, and
`TRADE-OFF` only as descriptive experiment labels.

## Future experiment success and falsification

Useful success does not mean “better.” Evidence may support the design if a
topology-aware policy removes lexical-spelling dependence, preserves K3 orbit
semantics, and produces reproducible role-correspondence differences while
still exposing trade-offs. That would still leave structural quality open.

The future run must evaluate:

- **H1:** richer topology roles eliminate at least some consequential lexical
  distinctions on Lighthouse/Ashen;
- **H2:** topology-aware semantics preserve K3 orbit equivalence;
- **H3:** a topology-derived order alone may remain insufficient because
  candidate index has no accepted quality meaning;
- **H4:** set-valued topology constraints preserve symmetry and isomorphism
  better than a universal literal total order;
- **H5:** multiple topology-aware witnesses may retain different profile
  classes;
- **H6:** if H5 holds, structural assignment objective or candidate-family
  redesign remains next;
- **H7:** lexical-spelling sensitivity and structural quality remain separate;
  and
- **H8:** the bounded experiment can select the next responsibility without
  selecting Production behavior.

Falsification boundaries are:

| N | Condition |
|---|---|
| N1 | prior topology roles cannot be reconstructed |
| N2 | classification requires names, IDs, or locale as semantics |
| N3 | K3 cannot be represented without a unique named role |
| N4 | candidate roles require visual preference to classify |
| N5 | every topology order is arbitrary and no bounded hypothesis is testable |
| N6 | correspondence cannot be stated without an undefined objective |
| N7 | execution requires best-mapping search |
| N8 | existing two-layer profile cannot compare policies |
| N9 | FP1 asymmetry makes assignment-only evidence uninterpretable |
| N10 | baseline/source drift |

N1 or N10 would block the future execution. N5 or N6 would redirect to
structural assignment-objective research rather than inventing an order.
