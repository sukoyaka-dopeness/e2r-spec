# LiaisonScape Representative Sample Release-Quality Acceptance 1

Date: 2026-09-15

## Classification

`C. PARTIAL / ACQUISITION-EVIDENCE GAP`

The inspected Representative Sample cases are usable on the Actual Product
surface, but Gate 1 is not closed for the complete canonical sample scope.
Ashen Crown has no current LiaisonScape normal-loader or development-only
acceptance-fixture acquisition path. Same-payload Save/reopen continuity was
also not established in this acceptance. These are evidence-scope gaps, not a
reason to change the Initial Layout provider or to treat the current research
residual as solved.

This is a release-facing Product acceptance checkpoint, not a new layout
algorithm or solver checkpoint. It evaluates whether representative samples
can be opened and inspected through the current LiaisonScape Product surface.

- production provider `NOT ESTABLISHED`;
- Product integration/default `HOLD`;
- research-candidate Human Review `NOT READY`;
- Initial Layout Release blocker `OPEN`.

No historical acceptance or Human Review evidence is inherited into this
matrix.

## Acceptance bar

The inspection checked:

- Dataset open and stable graph rendering;
- no blank/crash/navigation failure;
- no obvious Node-body overlap;
- understandable primary topology at the initial view;
- ordinary Reset view, zoom, and local inspection;
- EN/JA UI and sample presentation without mojibake;
- reload of a canonical acceptance fixture;
- distinction between Product-path observations and research stress residuals.

Overview density or the inability to read every Relation-label
simultaneously was not automatically classified as a failure when ordinary
zoom/local inspection remained usable. The known difficult relation/label
crowding is recorded as a bounded observation, not silently promoted to a
release blocker.

## Reload and Coordinate boundary

The Apollo 11 JA acceptance fixture was reloaded and returned to the same
Product graph surface. This verifies canonical fixture reload continuity, not
same-payload Save/reopen after a user coordinate edit. The latter remains
`UNCONFIRMED` because opening the canonical fixture again would not prove
reopening an in-memory saved payload.

All inspected canonical public samples are coordinate-less. The evidence
therefore covers current Product Derived placement. It does not establish a
stored-Coordinate fallback, and it does not authorize curated sample
Coordinates as a replacement for General Dataset Auto Layout.

## Actual Product smoke boundary

Screenshots and accessibility state were inspected from the actual Product
surface, not a diagnostic renderer. Lighthouse, Titanic, and Apollo all
reached stable graph views. Browser automation did not produce a reliable
pan-evidence artifact in this run, so pan is recorded as an available Product
control observed in the surface rather than as a formal movement assertion.

The central crowding in Titanic and Apollo did not produce a blank graph,
Node-body collision, mojibake, or navigation failure. It remains a
Relation-label/routing presentation observation and is not attributed to a
new Structural Placement defect by this checkpoint.

## Gate decision and next position

Gate 1 is `PARTIAL`, not `PASS` and not `NOT READY`:

- observed representative cases are usable with bounded follow-up;
- complete canonical representative coverage is missing for Ashen Crown;
- same-payload Save/reopen is unconfirmed;
- no ordinary representative catastrophic failure was found;
- difficult relation/label crowding remains visible but is not by itself a
  proof that the Hub must be stopped.

The next bounded action is to decide whether an acceptance-only Ashen Crown
fixture seam is warranted and, if so, verify Ashen Crown EN/JA with the same
matrix. Do not add Ashen Crown to the normal Product sample loader solely for
this acceptance. After Gate 1 is closed or explicitly bounded, proceed to the
separate `General / Complex Dataset Practicality Gate`.

Do not start that Gate 2 measurement, deep occupied-geometry research,
Relation-label solver refinement, HQ metric refinement, solver portfolio work,
or Adaptive Initial Placement Cascade in this checkpoint.

No knowledge candidate is added: the result is a release-specific acceptance
matrix and acquisition boundary, not a reusable cross-repository principle.
