## Decision

The current `coarse-objective-prototype-v1` should be **STOP / PARKED as a
Product-default adoption candidate**. It remains available through the
explicit opt-in research seam. This is not a rejection of the bounded
provider's safety or integration contract, and it does not authorize changing
the Product default.

The reason is evidentiary: the Post candidate was preferred over current
placement in the earlier comparison cells, while direct current-versus-coarse
Actual Product observation found only mixed/insufficient coarse benefit. The
coarse proxy therefore does not yet explain or reproduce the observed Post
benefit strongly enough to justify default adoption.

## Gap attribution

### Responsibilities that belong to Initial Layout

- Node positions and body clearance;
- bounded candidate generation and movement/search freedom;
- cheap estimated occupancy and label envelopes;
- coarse geometric proxies for crossings, edge extent, parallel pressure, and
  Relation-label corridors; and
- deterministic Derived output with bounded work and fallback.

### Downstream responsibilities that must not be absorbed

- routed Edge geometry and obstacle recovery;
- actual Relation-label placement and collision handling;
- Self-loop angle, radius, route, and label placement;
- connector rendering and layering; and
- actual glyph shaping, CSS layout, and locale rendering.

The Post implementation and evidence use signals that can be strongly coupled
to these downstream presentation stages. A lower coarse proxy score cannot be
treated as proof of the same visual improvement. In particular, Apollo's
unsafe coarse candidate and fallback provide non-regression evidence only, not
coarse visual benefit.

## Evidence synthesis

- The coarse provider is finite, complete, Derived, bounded, and safe for
  completed candidates, with whole-result current fallback.
- Stored/mixed/coordinate-less authority, dirty-state, Save Coordinates, and
  same-payload reopen semantics remain valid.
- Lighthouse and Titanic EN/JA Actual Product comparisons showed usable
  output, but no consistent human-observable improvement over current.
- Existing cross-fixture Post comparisons show a stronger research reference,
  but do not establish Post equivalence for coarse or authorize a runtime port.
- Machine proxy reductions on Lighthouse/Titanic are useful diagnostic signals,
  not visual superiority evidence.
- Known parallel Edge/long Relation-label, Self-loop angle, and connector
  behavior remain separate presentation tracks.

## Research direction

Do not add more weights, movement radius, iterations, or fixture-specific
signals to the current coarse objective solely to chase the Post observations.
That would risk optimizing a proxy whose causal gap is not established.

If research resumes, it should begin with a new explicit hypothesis, such as a
controlled attribution study that measures the same initial positions through
the existing routing/label pipeline, or a separately designed cheap
presentation approximation. It must preserve the existing authority boundary
and compare against current and Post without claiming equivalence.

No additional human visual review is required to close this decision: the
available human evidence is sufficient to establish mixed/insufficient coarse
benefit, while not showing a coarse-specific regression.
