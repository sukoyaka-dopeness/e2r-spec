# LiaisonScape Initial-Layout Product Adoption Decision 1

Date: 2026-09-11

Status: HOLD — no Product default change authorized

## Decision

The explicit decision for adopting `coarse-objective-prototype-v1` as the
Product default coordinate-less initial-placement provider is **HOLD**.

This is not a rejection of the bounded provider or its opt-in integration.
The provider contract, Actual Product opt-in human acceptance, persistence
authority, fallback non-regression boundary, and bounded work contract are
accepted for the tested scope. The evidence is not sufficient to make this
research prototype the default authority.

## Evidence basis

- Actual Product opt-in human acceptance is CLOSED/PASS for Lighthouse,
  Titanic EN/JA, Apollo EN/JA fallback, interaction, and same-payload reopen.
- Stored, mixed, and coordinate-less authority semantics remain correct.
- Completed coarse candidates are finite, complete, Derived, and body-safe.
- Unsafe or timeout cases return the existing Product placement as a complete
  whole-result fallback.
- Large/dense inputs are bounded, but fallback geometry inherits the current
  Product solver and does not establish a new clearance guarantee.
- Existing research evidence preferred the Post candidate on the observed
  comparison cells. `coarse-objective-prototype-v1` is not equivalent to Post,
  and no universal visual superiority claim is established.
- Known routing, Relation-label, Self-loop, and connector presentation issues
  remain separate tracks.

Machine metrics and stress tests support safety and boundedness; they do not
replace human visual acceptance or establish default visual quality.

## Boundary for a future adoption implementation

If a later checkpoint revisits adoption, it must preserve at least:

- stored Coordinates as authoritative;
- existing mixed-coordinate completion semantics;
- coarse targeting only for coordinate-less Datasets;
- Derived placement without automatic Dataset mutation or dirty state;
- explicit drag plus Save Coordinates as the persistence path;
- exact current Product placement for unsafe/timeout fallback;
- existing routing, labels, connector, viewport, locale, and interaction
  authorities; and
- the distinct provider identity `coarse-objective-prototype-v1` unless a
  separate identity decision is made.

That future implementation must be a separate checkpoint. This record does
not authorize it, and does not authorize fallback geometry repair, App.tsx
refactoring, Post-equivalence claims, or publication.
