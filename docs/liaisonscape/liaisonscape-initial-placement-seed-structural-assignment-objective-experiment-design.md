## Exact normalized-altitude axis

The accepted axis is the normalized minimum altitude to the opposite chord in
one motif. For triangle vertices A, B, and C, the altitude from A is:

~~~text
h_A = abs(cross(B-A, C-A)) / length(C-B)
~~~

The accepted normalized minimum view divides the minimum altitude by the
longest triangle side. Since the minimum altitude is opposite a longest side,
the same accepted view can be compared as:

~~~text
normalizedAltitude(A,B,C)
  = 2 * abs(cross(B-A, C-A)) / L_max^2
~~~

where L_max^2 is the maximum exact squared side length. This is an algebraic
rearrangement of the accepted altitude/chord definition, not a new axis.

With rational FP1 coordinates, the numerator and denominator are rational.
The longest side is selected by exact squared-length comparison. For two
hard-valid assignments on the same transported motif, compare exact rational
cross-products:

~~~text
alt_X_num * alt_Y_den
vs
alt_Y_num * alt_X_den
~~~

GEO-0 excludes zero-area triangles before preference. Equality is exact
cross-product equality. No square root, epsilon, decimal threshold, or
unchecked fixed-width arithmetic is semantic authority. Arbitrary precision,
symbolic arithmetic, or checked bounds are required conceptually.

ALTITUDE-EXACT-READY holds for the bounded exact-rational FP1 experiment.
Human-readable decimals are descriptive aids only.

For fixed topology, family, stage, representation, and complete coverage:

- PP-PREF-EQUIVALENT means all applicable registered records are equal;
- PP-X-DOMINATES-Y means X is no worse on every applicable record, strictly
  better on at least one, and no required record is unknown;
- PP-Y-DOMINATES-X is symmetric;
- PP-INCOMPARABLE means at least one record favors each side;
- PP-UNKNOWN means a required record cannot legally be compared; and
- PP-NOT-APPLICABLE means no registered record applies.

not become undeclared axes.
