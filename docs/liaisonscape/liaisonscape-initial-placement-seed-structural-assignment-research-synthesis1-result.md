## 2. Bounded research question

The research question was:

> Within the explicitly bounded TA0–TA3 structural-assignment lineage, can the
> candidate-generation, assignment/correspondence semantics, operational
> witnesses, and PP-M3 objective relations be reconciled without silently
> turning a baseline or a fixture-local literal relation into a universal
> policy winner?

This question is narrower than “which initial placement looks best?” and
narrower than “has initial placement been solved?”. It asks whether the
research artifacts form an internally auditable chain and whether an additional
same-level objective experiment is required by an unresolved evidence gap.

## 3. Ten-layer evidence model

The synthesis keeps the following layers separate.

1. **Problem/question** — what bounded research question is being answered.
2. **Hard constraints** — non-negotiable geometric and safety floors.
3. **Candidate space** — the generated coordinate families and their scope.
4. **Assignment/correspondence semantics** — how roles, motifs, and candidate
   identities are assigned or mapped.
5. **Operational witnesses** — executable or reconstructed materializers and
   their provenance.
6. **Objective contract** — the permitted partial preference relation, not an
   invented scalar score.
7. **Exact evidence** — hashes, counts, exact relations, and fixture-local
   outputs.
8. **Interpretation** — what those exact outputs mean within scope.
9. **Rejected/unsupported claims** — conclusions the evidence cannot support.
10. **Current-scope closure and pre-Production gaps** — what is closed and the
    smallest remaining responsibility.

Conflating these layers is the main way a hard-valid candidate can be
mistaken for a structurally preferred candidate, or a structurally preferred
literal witness can be mistaken for a visually good Production policy.

## 4. Hard constraints and candidate space

### 4.1 Hard safety floor

The accepted hard floor is limited to the following bounded statements:

- A nondegenerate motif triangle must be non-collinear.
- Motif-neighbor rays from one shared Node must not occupy the same positive
  directed ray when a noncoincident realization exists.
- GEO-0 and GEO-3 remain hard geometric floors.

Passing this floor means **hard-valid** only. It does not imply **structurally
preferred**, and neither implies **visually good**.

### 4.2 FP1-NGP candidate family

The FP1-NGP family is defined by

```text
P_i = (i/m, (i/m)^2)
Q_i = (m*i, i^2)
```

The exact `Q_i` form is the arithmetic substrate used by the bounded
candidate-family work. The research established deterministic candidate roles,
hard-floor realizability, and exact arithmetic handling. It did not establish
visual quality, optimality, Production suitability, or universal superiority.

## 5. TA0–TA3 lineage reconciliation

### 5.1 TA0 baseline

TA0 is `WIT-DERIVED`: degree-descending order followed by lexical identity
direct zip. It is a baseline/regression anchor. It is not canonical, neutral,
ground truth, or a winner.

### 5.2 TA1

TA1 semantics are recorded separately from `TA1-W1-OP-v1`. `TA1-W1-OP-v1` is
new operational evidence with `CLAIM-P0` provenance. It is not a historical
replay, policy representative, or neutral witness. The provenance distinction
is intentional and prevents an operational reconstruction from being silently
presented as the lost historical implementation.

### 5.3 TA2

The historical exact TA2 materializers were not recovered. The current
research therefore uses new, explicitly named witnesses:

- `TA2-DEG-NEW-v1` and `TA2-MOTIF-NEW-v1` are new semantics/materializers.
- `TA2-DEG-W1-OP-v1` and `TA2-MOTIF-W1-OP-v1` are their operational witnesses.
- The responsibility tuple is
  `R(V) = (m(V), o(V), s(V))`, ordered componentwise by the partial-order
  semantics.

There is a separate known semantic-design observation: under the current
overlap-cluster definition, every motif containing `V` shares `V`. Therefore
`o(V)=0` when `m(V)=0`, and `o(V)=1` when `m(V)>0`; `o(V)` is order-redundant in
current v1. This is a follow-up design issue, not an invalidation of the
current witnesses or their provenance.

### 5.4 TA3

Historical TA3 recovery exhausted the high-level intent, but did not recover
the exact historical responsibility tuple, role ordering, tie rules,
transformation, mappings, or runner. The current new semantics are explicitly
named:

- `TA3-RESP-NEW-v1`
- `TA3-SPACE-NEW-v1`
- `TA3-CORR-FWD-NEW-v1`
- `TA3-CORR-REV-NEW-v1`
- `TA3-CORR-OP-v1`

The operational witnesses are `TA3-FWD-W1-OP-v1` and
`TA3-REV-W1-OP-v1`. All are `TA3W-MAN-5` witnesses. TA3 therefore contributes
new, bounded correspondence evidence without becoming historical replay or a
Production policy.

### 5.5 Geometry-to-family keying correction

The earlier 4/4/2 discrepancy came from a derived identity-audit row-order
