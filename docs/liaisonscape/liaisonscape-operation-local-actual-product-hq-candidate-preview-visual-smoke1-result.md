## Preview boundary

The development-only seam is placed at the App's Node-geometry input to its
existing presentation derivation. Candidate positions live in isolated React
preview state; the normal session `positions`, Dataset, coordinate ownership,
dirty state, and Save Coordinates input are not modified. Current Product
routing, Parallel / Incident allocation, endpoint planning, Relation-label and
Node-label placement, Self-loop routing, feedback, viewport fitting, and styles
remain the rendering authorities.

The preview requires exact operation ID, generation, semantic snapshot
identity, complete Entity IDs, finite positions, and candidate fingerprint.
Mismatch fails closed. The graph is read-only while previewing. Explicit End
restores the Fast geometry; Home and Dataset-open paths discard the preview.
The evidence URL consumes its one-shot preview token with `replaceState`, so a
reload returns to Fast geometry. No accept/revert or persistence path is used.

## Actual Product visual smoke

Three exact selected fingerprints from Worker Product Metric Parity 1 were
replayed on the Actual Product graph surface and compared with Fast Initial on
the same surface.

- Canonical, 8 Nodes / 10 Relations: no Entity overlap or obvious presentation
  breakage. Parallel lanes remained distinguishable. HQ and Fast geometry were
  nearly identical, so no meaningful improvement was demonstrated.
- Dense cheap-equivalent witness, 14 / 49: failed. At about 48% fit, Relation
  labels crowded every cluster, route/label ownership was difficult to follow,
  and graph legibility was not acceptable despite the candidate being the
  metric-selected finalist.
- Japanese label-heavy, 10 / 20: failed. At about 54% fit, long Japanese Node
  and Relation labels overlapped extensively and were not readable.

The exact prior Worker campaign had no Self-loop candidate, so Self-loop visual
quality is not claimed. The canonical/dense/label-heavy surfaces were the real
App renderer, not a diagnostic SVG. Fixed candidate replay was immediate; this
does not resolve the prior load-sensitive dense Worker wall time.

## Decision

The preview architecture is safe enough for bounded visual evidence, but the
candidate family and HQ metric do not yet support Product visual readiness.
Dense and long-label failures are a direct `METRIC / VISUAL QUALITY GAP`.
Formal Human Review would add cost without a viable candidate and remains
`NOT READY`.
