## Purpose and boundary

This document prepares the second bounded Hybrid reconstruction pilot after
[Product Verification Pilot Result 1](roadmap-hybrid-reconstruction-product-verification-pilot-result1.md).
It applies the same claim-level method:

- **B where proven**: a current planning consequence has a clear latest
  authority, while dated detail can be delegated to the existing broad
  chronology;
- **A where ambiguous**: the heading and live boundary remain, but the body
  may later be reduced to current consequence plus authority pointers; and
- **HOLD where unresolved**: status, authority, scope, or implementation
  meaning is not safe to collapse without another Human decision.

No `docs/roadmap.md` or Roadmap History content is changed by this
preparation. The group labels below are manifest labels for the next pilot,
not a new documentation taxonomy.

The current E2R-SPEC baseline is commit
`9771b40add3d151aa2b35e1b51c7765a85ecb070` (`docs: correct verification
pilot measurements`). The first pilot is complete for PV-01-PV-10; its result
keeps PV-11-PV-14 outside scope and explicitly separates quality-solver,
screening/probing, provider-shaped execution, and Worker/application
responsibilities.

Scaling Envelope / Main-thread Viability 1 (2026-09-14)`.

preserved semantic equivalence, the largest tested case was 24 Nodes / 80

the dense-large Node-label phase transition exceeds the preferred reference,

compression; HOLD for a combined pilot**.

disposition and its dedicated result pointer. It must not merge these claims
into G2-A or present the diagnostic selector as Product adoption. If a future
pilot needs to remove or combine headings, the exact target-rule and

pattern: one current Roadmap synthesis, with dated scaling measurements and
fixture chronology delegated to the existing broad History. The final edit
must recheck the exact headings and inbound references before staging; any

- choose a quality solver, candidate winner, provider, Worker transport, or
  Product default;

- change Initial Layout priority, blocker, execution order, or current
  coordination authority.

with G2-A unless its live risk and target boundaries are independently
reconciled. G2-C Worker/application adoption and G2-D visual quality remain
