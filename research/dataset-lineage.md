# Research Topics

This directory contains exploratory ideas for the future evolution of the E2R ecosystem.

These documents are not part of the Core specification and do not define normative behavior.

Instead, they record research directions that may influence future specifications and applications.

---

# Current Research Topics

## Historical Ecosystem

Investigates how multiple historical datasets with different viewpoints can coexist within the E2R ecosystem.

Topics include:

- Multiple historical perspectives
- Historical and fictional datasets
- Viewpoint-dependent confidence
- Cross-dataset comparison
- Historical debate visualization

Document:

- historical-ecosystem.md

---

## Dataset Identity

Explores how applications may identify and manage multiple datasets without expanding the E2R Core specification.

Topics include:

- Dataset identifiers
- Local vs global identity
- Merge strategies
- Dataset references
- Identifier collision avoidance

Document:

- dataset-identity.md

# Philosophy

Research documents are intentionally exploratory.

Ideas described here may change, be merged into specifications, move to application design documents, or be abandoned as the ecosystem evolves.
