# Multiverse

## Purpose

This document explores the possibility that multiple E2R datasets may represent independent worlds rather than independent files.

This is an exploratory research topic and is not part of the current E2R specification.

---

# Motivation

Current E2R datasets are independent.

However, in practice, multiple datasets may describe different versions of the same world.

Examples include:

* Alternative historical interpretations
* Fictional timelines
* Simulation branches
* User-specific knowledge spaces
* AI-generated hypothetical worlds

Instead of treating these as isolated datasets, they may be viewed as parallel universes.

---

# Dataset as Universe

Each dataset may define its own universe.

Within a universe:

* Entities exist.
* Events occur.
* Relations connect objects.

No assumptions are made about other universes.

---

# Independent Evolution

Universes may evolve independently.

Possible differences include:

* different histories
* different entities
* different laws
* different extensions

A change in one universe does not imply changes in another.

---

# Shared Origins

Different universes may originate from the same ancestor dataset.

For example:

* Dataset A
* Dataset A'
* Dataset A''

Each branch evolves independently after divergence.

This resembles version control while allowing semantic divergence rather than simple document revision.

---

# Cross-Universe Relationships

Future research may explore relationships between universes.

Examples include:

* common ancestors
* synchronization
* comparison
* merging
* translation
* correspondence

These relationships are intentionally outside the current Core specification.

---

# Observer Perspective

Applications may choose to observe:

* a single universe
* multiple universes
* differences between universes

Different applications may therefore present different views without modifying the underlying datasets.

---

# Relationship to Branch Evolution

Branch evolution describes how datasets diverge over time.

The multiverse perspective instead focuses on treating each branch as a complete world with its own internal consistency.

The two concepts are complementary.
