# Names Grouping — Candidate Comparison Disposition

Date: 2026-08-14

## Verdict

`accept`

The candidate comparison is accepted as a responsibility-level evaluation.
The five common preservation tests are sufficient and closed; no further
semantic fixtures are required at this stage.

## Candidate boundary

The comparison remains among three neutral directions:

- Names-scoped record-like fact;
- Names-scoped association-like grouping fact; and
- Names-scoped optional packaged data.

No direction is selected. P1 expression identity remains the baseline, and
P2/P3 are not reopened.

The principal risk remains distinct for each direction: record-like may imply
Group identity, association-like may imply relation/graph semantics, and
optional packaged data may imply capability/profile ownership. These are risks
to guard against, not grounds for selecting or excluding a candidate.

## Deferred semantics

Cardinality, duplicate membership, Group equality, symmetry, transitivity,
direction, targetability, Group identity, lifecycle, Payload, Extension ID,
migration, and application behavior remain deferred.
