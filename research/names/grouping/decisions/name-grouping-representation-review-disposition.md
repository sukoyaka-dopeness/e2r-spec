# Names Grouping Representation Boundary — Review Disposition

Date: 2026-08-14

## Verdict

`accept with corrections`

The boundary is sufficient for the next representation comparison after the
following guardrails were added.

## Accepted corrections

- Minimum durable meaning is intentional membership plus distinct rationale;
  provenance remains separate.
- One rationale's membership does not imply another rationale's membership.
- Unrelated P1 or Dataset edits must not erase opaque grouping.
- Substantive P1 replacement does not silently retarget grouping membership.
- Unsupported grouping is distinct from an understood grouping with a missing
  or unresolved P1 expression target.
- Durable grouping does not itself require an independently targetable Group
  identity.
- Grouping semantics are Names-scoped; packaging ownership remains deferred.

## Deferred

Record/relation/profile choice, Group ID, independent grouping identity,
cardinality, vocabulary, provenance, authorship, timestamps, lifecycle,
cleanup, cross-Dataset equality, Claim/Evidence/Assessment targeting, Payload,
Extension ID, migration, and application behavior remain undecided.
