# Names Grouping — Selection Reopen Criteria

Date: 2026-08-14

Status: Deferred-selection control; non-normative

## Reopen trigger

Reopen representation selection only when new evidence demonstrates that one
candidate direction expresses the same Names-scoped grouping fact with
materially less semantic risk and no loss of the five common preservation
boundaries.

## Acceptable evidence

Evidence must be comparative and concrete, such as a materially different
consumer or interoperability workflow that:

- requires a distinction one candidate preserves more directly;
- cannot be represented equivalently by the other two without introducing
  accidental semantics or interoperability loss; and
- passes overlapping-rationale, missing-target, opaque round-trip,
  non-retargeting, and unrelated-edit preservation checks.

Convenience, implementation familiarity, smaller syntax, or presumed future
features are not sufficient evidence.

## Explicit exclusions

Do not reopen selection merely to decide cardinality, Group equality,
duplicate membership, empty/single-member behavior, Payload syntax, Extension
ID, migration, or application writer behavior. Those remain deferred until a
valid candidate direction is established.
