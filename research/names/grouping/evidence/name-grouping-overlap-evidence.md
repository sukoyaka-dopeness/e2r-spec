# Names Grouping — Overlapping Rationale Evidence

Date: 2026-08-14

Status: Research evidence checkpoint; non-normative

## Purpose

Test whether one P1 Name expression can participate in more than one grouping
rationale without making those groupings an identity equivalence class.

## Case

For Entity `E1`, expression `N2 = Tokyo` may participate in:

- a current multilingual/transliteration family with `東京` and `Tōkyō`; and
- a catalog export family that groups the preferred Latin rendering with a
  particular external catalog context.

These are different facts. Membership in one must not imply membership in the
other, preferred display, historical applicability, or replacement.

## Boundary

This evidence does not define grouping cardinality, group IDs, payload shape,
or relation semantics. It only requires readers to preserve the rationale
distinction and the independent P1 expression identity.
